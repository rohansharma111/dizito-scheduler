Architecture after the planned refactor

Once you replace the platform-specific columns with a provider_metadata JSONB field, this blueprint should remain unchanged. The only difference will be that platform-specific identifiers (page IDs, board IDs, location IDs, etc.) are stored in provider_metadata instead of individual columns, making future platform integrations (X, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, etc.) require little or no database schema changes.
